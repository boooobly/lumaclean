# LumaClean SEO checkpoints — 30/60/90 days

Baseline date: 21 September 2026. This is a measurement plan, not a traffic or ranking promise. Compare only complete periods and separate branded from non-branded queries.

Latest execution checkpoint: [29 September](SEO_CHECKPOINT_2026-09-29.md). Yandex has processed all 63 sitemap URLs, but excluded several RU guides as low-demand. Journal navigation, the pricing guide and missing hub social metadata were improved; Google office recrawl is still pending. Historical baseline dates below are unchanged.

## First 30 days

- Recheck the URLs submitted to Google on 20 September after crawl dates have had time to change. Do not resubmit unchanged URLs that remain queued.
- Recheck the five Russian service pages in Yandex Webmaster and record the new crawl/exclusion dates before requesting another crawl.
- In Search Console, compare queries and landing pages for Serbian apartment and office cleaning. Keep impressions, clicks, CTR and average position together; do not optimize from one metric alone.
- Reconcile each real enquiry by its `LC-...` number. Record source/landing from the Telegram message, whether the request was qualified, whether it became an agreed booking, and whether the work was completed.
- Review production Core Web Vitals and mobile errors. A lab score is diagnostic; field data is the decision signal.

## By 60 days

- Update titles, descriptions, FAQs or internal links only where the query-to-page report shows a repeated non-brand intent and enough impressions to compare periods.
- Check whether service pages, article hubs and priority articles are gaining indexed coverage. Separate crawl/indexing changes from ranking changes.
- Compare mobile versus desktop enquiries and form errors. Investigate a funnel step only when the difference repeats across more than a handful of sessions.
- Add real work photographs and verified customer proof if the owner can supply them with publication rights. Do not substitute stock images as evidence of completed work.
- Decide whether the prepared same-day article should be published based on actual availability and owner approval; do not publish it merely to increase URL count.

## By 90 days

- Compare the latest complete 28-day Search Console period with the preceding complete 28 days: non-brand clicks, impressions, CTR, average position and landing-page distribution.
- Report the commercial funnel separately: enquiries, qualified enquiries, agreed bookings and completed jobs. Do not treat messenger clicks or `generate_lead` as completed orders.
- Keep, merge or retire new content ideas according to demonstrated query demand and operational fit. Avoid district pages or service variants unsupported by real delivery.
- Review local profiles and consistent contact details. Add only legitimate directories and profiles that can be maintained; do not fabricate an office address, reviews or operating history.
- Set the next quarter's priorities from the strongest combination of search demand, conversion quality and the team's ability to fulfil the service.

## Minimum reporting fields

| Area | Fields |
|---|---|
| Search | engine, period, query, landing page, brand/non-brand, clicks, impressions, CTR, average position |
| Indexing | URL, engine, state, last crawl, canonical, action/date |
| Lead | `LC-...` reference, date, locale, entry source, landing page, qualified yes/no |
| Commercial | agreed booking yes/no, completed yes/no, reason lost, service type |
| Experience | route, device, LCP, INP, CLS, console/runtime errors |

Personal details stay in the operational Telegram conversation and must not be copied into analytics or technical reports.
