LumaClean's ordinary three-seat sofa at 3,000 RSD is within the mainstream sample, slightly above the middle of explicit fixed standard-sofa quotes. The 2,400 dvosed, 1,200 armchair, 500 chair and 900 corner seat are consistent with this positioning. The light sofa catalogue entry at 2,500 is always accompanied by the standalone minimum of 3,000. A stain added to existing cleaning starts at 1,500; a standalone stain visit totals at least 3,000 including basic treatment. Whole-sofa treatment never adds a second equipment or stain fee for the same work.
